Projects

Case studies

Problems I took on at Algoma University, what I did about them, and how they turned out.

Algoma University IT · Excel, PowerPoint

Cutting print costs by 32%

Problem
Print and supply costs across shared campus devices were high, and it was not clear where the volume came from.
Action
Analyzed usage data from multiple devices in Excel, using XLOOKUP and data summary tools to find the main sources of print volume and page counts, then presented the findings at a monthly department meeting.
Result
The department blocked unauthorized full-book printing, set per-print page limits, replaced the $50 per-semester allowance with a $20 rechargeable balance, and raised the per-page price. Print and supply costs fell by 32%.
