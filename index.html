About

Technical support, grounded in business sense

I’m an IT support professional and a Bachelor of Business Administration (Honours, Cum Laude) graduate of Algoma University. From May 2024 to August 2025 I worked as a Service Desk Assistant co-op in the university’s 20-person IT department, supporting a campus of roughly 5,000 students along with faculty and staff.

Day to day, that meant resolving tier-1 tickets in Freshservice, managing accounts in Active Directory and Google Admin, capturing and deploying laptop images over PXE, onboarding new staff each semester, and keeping classroom AV running. My business training shapes how I work: I look at the data, find where time and money are being lost, and bring a clear recommendation to the table.

Before IT, I hosted live radio on HITS FM 91.2 in Kathmandu and worked the front counter at Copper Branch in Mississauga. Both taught me to communicate clearly and stay calm under pressure. I’m also an award-winning competitive debater.
